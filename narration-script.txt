[INTRO]
Imagine a condition affecting hundreds of millions of adults.
About 1 in 10 adults worldwide has prediabetes.
Most don't know it, and there are often no symptoms.

[GAUGE]
Prediabetes is higher-than-normal blood sugar, but not yet diabetes.
The HbA1C blood test shows where you stand.
5.7 to 6.4 percent is prediabetes.
And 6.5 percent or higher is diabetes.

[BODY]
So what's happening inside the body?
Normally, insulin is a key that lets sugar into your cells.
But sometimes your cells stop responding to it.
So sugar stays in your blood, and blood sugar rises.

[RISKS]
Some risk factors are out of your control.
Others come from daily habits, and you can change these.
Certain health conditions also raise your risk.
More risk factors mean a higher chance of prediabetes and type 2 diabetes.
But high risk is a warning, not a diagnosis.

[TESTS]
The HbA1C shows your average blood sugar over two to three months.
A fasting test checks it after not eating overnight.
A glucose tolerance test checks it after a sugary drink.
If you have risk factors, ask your doctor about testing.

[GOOD]
Lifestyle changes cut the risk of type 2 diabetes by 58 percent in a major trial.
Lose 5 to 7 percent of your weight.
Move 150 minutes a week.
Eat more whole foods.

[CLOSE]
Learn your risk factors. Talk to your doctor about a blood test.
Move more, eat well, and sleep well.
Our goal is to spread community awareness and educate people about prediabetes.
This video is for education, not medical advice.
